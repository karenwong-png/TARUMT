M Adora · Wangsa Melawati
M Adora
~ closest to campus, low density, walk to class ~
Mah Sing’s M Adora rises in two 31-storey towers over Jalan Wangsa Melawati 1, 677 units handed over ahead of schedule in 2024 on a low-density 4.5-acre parcel. Thirty-five facilities crown it — an Olympic-length horizon pool, a cantilevered glass gymnasium fitted out with Technogym, and a sky bridge strung across Level 29 with the Melawati hills beyond. At 1.6 km it is the closer of the two addresses to campus: about twenty minutes on foot, five by car. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive.
MH Platinum 2 · Setapak
MH Platinum 2
~ newest keys, courtyard living, LRT-linked ~
The newer keys of the two. MH Group and Platinum Victory arranged three 40-storey blocks and 1,254 units around a central courtyard on 4.72 acres of Jalan Usahawan 6, completed in June 2025. The facility list reads like a resort brief — swimming and spa pools, sauna, yoga deck, hammock garden, sunset deck and a jogging track threaded through the landscaping. Setapak Central, AEON Alpha Angle and Wangsa Maju LRT are minutes away; campus is 2.0 km north along Jalan Genting Klang. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive.

Getting Around

Getting to TAR UMT

The TAR UMT main campus sits on Jalan Genting Kelang in Setapak, a 171-acre site between two LRT stations — Wangsa Maju on the Kelana Jaya Line is about a nine-minute walk from the main entrance, and campus shuttle buses call there through the day. Pick a residence to see how far it is from campus, which station serves it, and how long the trip usually takes.
